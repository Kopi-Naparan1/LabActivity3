About K-FIT

K-FIT is a fitness center on Sayre Highway in Valencia City, Bukidnon. We offer strength training, combat sports, and Pilates for people of all ages and fitness levels.

Our goal is simple: give you a clean space, experienced coaches, and a community that keeps you showing up.
